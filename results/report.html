Harmonic / Percussive Decomposition with Explicit Uncertainty

Generated by scripts/run_study.py. Sample rate 22050 Hz, durations up to 4.0 s. Audio stems and mask figures are linked from each row.

What is and is not claimed. This is a structural harmonic/percussive separator driven by time- and frequency-direction median supports, not a source classifier. It does not label instruments and does not assume that "high band = one instrument, low band = another"; two parts are separated only when their short-time morphology differs. The "clean" scenarios satisfy that assumption. The "counterexample" scenarios deliberately violate it (vibrato, breathy noise, overlapping onsets, glissando, modulated drone) and are expected to produce large cross-talk; their rows show that rather than hiding it. Ambiguous bins are routed by an explicit rule and are never deleted: the delivered harmonic + percussive + residual stems always remix to the input (see recomposition-error column), and no stem is renormalized.

2. Ambiguity policy comparison

Identical supports, three rules for bins claimed by both structures. residual (default) moves the ambiguous fraction to the residual stem, so harmonic+percussive+residual still equals the input. shared deliberately assigns the ambiguous fraction to both stems; its mask sum then exceeds 1 and the remix overshoots by the reported amount — this is shown on purpose so the duplication is measurable rather than implicit. complementary ignores ambiguity and is the usual soft-mask baseline.

5. How to read the numbers

  • mean SDR: mir_eval BSS SDR, which rescales estimates, so it measures shape only. delivered vs input dB is the unscaled level check.
  • H/P SIR: interference rejection; the direct cross-talk measure.
  • crosstalk fraction: time-domain projection of a stem onto the other true source. unexplained energy is not attributed to either source (a masking nonlinearity or a source that is not a scaled version of the truth).
  • recomposition err: −∞ means the three stems summed exactly to the input (magnitude masks with phase preserved). A finite value with policy shared is the intentional duplication, not a bug.
  • mask F1: agreement with an oracle ideal-ratio mask. For level-imbalanced mixtures this is dominated by the loud source and is not a correctness probability.

Machine-readable metrics: results/metrics/report.json, results/metrics/summary.csv.
